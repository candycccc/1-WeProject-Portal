1. ABOUT THIS NOTICE

WeProject is a customer and project portal. A business you are dealing with — its installer, integrator or contractor — uses it to send you quotations, share project information and documents, record your approvals and, in some cases, take payment.

This notice explains what happens to your information in WeProject. It is short on purpose. For the matters that apply across everything we do — security, international transfers, cookies and your rights in full — it refers to the Ouitech Ltd Privacy Policy at wequote.cloud/privacy-policy, which should be read alongside this notice.

2. TWO ORGANISATIONS ARE INVOLVED, AND THEY HAVE DIFFERENT ROLES

This is the most important thing to understand about your information in WeProject.

The Business is responsible for its relationship with you. It decides why it holds information about you, what information it needs, what it quotes, what it records about your project, what documents it asks for, and how long it keeps its business records. In data protection terms it is the controller for that information.

We provide the technology. Where we handle information about you in order to make the Portal work for the Business, we do so on the Business’s instructions and under a written contract with it. In data protection terms we are its processor. We do not use that information for our own purposes.

We are responsible for some things directly. We are the controller for a limited set of activities that are ours rather than the Business’s: creating and securing your Portal account, authenticating you, protecting the platform against fraud and abuse, keeping the service running, responding to you if you contact our support team, and meeting our own legal obligations.

What this means for you. If you want to know why a business holds information about you, or you want it corrected or deleted, contact that business first. Its own privacy notice will explain how it uses your information. Section 10 explains what we can and cannot do ourselves.

3. WHERE YOUR INFORMATION CAME FROM

You may have received an invitation to WeProject before you had any account with us. That is because the Business gave us your details in order to set up your record and invite you.

Information about you reaches us:

  • from the Business — typically your name, company, email address, telephone number, role and a customer reference, provided so that it can create your record, invite you to the Portal, send you a quotation, manage a project or communicate with you;
  • from you — when you activate your account, update your details, upload a document, respond to a quotation, add comments, submit a form, make a payment or contact support;
  • automatically — technical and security information generated when you use the Portal, described in section 4; and
  • from Stripe, in relation to the outcome of a payment.

4. WHAT INFORMATION IS PROCESSED

Your account. Name, email address, telephone number, company, job title, username, customer or account reference, and authentication information including your password in hashed form and session data.

Quotations and proposals. Quotation references, the products or services quoted, quantities, pricing, discounts, specifications, notes, proposal documents, quotation status, your approval or rejection, and related messages.

Project information. Project name or reference, status, requirements, milestones, schedules, tasks, requested information, products and services, project documentation, approvals, your comments and other project-related information.

Documents and files. Documents that you or the Business upload, which depending on the project may include specifications, drawings, photographs, site information, contracts, quotation documents, invoices and supporting paperwork.

Payments. Where the Business has enabled payments — see section 7.

Portal activity. Records of what happens in the Portal: account creation, login activity, quotation views, document access, uploads, submissions, approvals, acceptances, rejections, confirmations, other project actions, and the dates and times of each.

Technical and security information. IP address, device information, browser type, operating system, login and session information, access times, security events, system logs and error information. IP addresses count as personal information, and we treat them as such.

Support. Emails, support requests, messages, feedback and related correspondence.

5. DOCUMENTS YOU UPLOAD

Please upload only what the Business has asked for.

WeProject is not designed or intended for sensitive information — information about health, racial or ethnic origin, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, genetic or biometric information, sexual orientation, or criminal offences. Please do not include such information in documents you upload. If the Business needs information of that kind from you, it should collect it another way.

Our contract with the Business requires it to observe the same limits.

6. HOW YOUR INFORMATION IS USED

To provide the Portal — creating and managing your account, authenticating you, giving you secure access, connecting you with the right Business, displaying quotations and proposals, showing project information, sharing documents, receiving documents and information from you, recording your approvals and responses, and supporting communication.

To manage quotations and projects, on the Business’s instructions — presenting quotations, recording your responses, managing project information, maintaining project records and enabling the two of you to work together.

To keep the Portal secure — authenticating users, protecting accounts, identifying unauthorised access, detecting suspicious activity, preventing fraud and abuse, and investigating security incidents.

To support and maintain the service — providing technical support, investigating errors, responding to enquiries, monitoring performance and keeping the Portal reliable.

To meet legal obligations and protect legal rights — complying with the law, responding to lawful requests, establishing, exercising or defending legal claims, enforcing our agreements and investigating misuse.

Where we act as a controller, we rely on the performance of a contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Portal, compliance with legal obligations, and, where the law requires it, your consent. Section 6 of the Ouitech Ltd Privacy Policy sets this out in full.

7. PAYMENTS

Where the Business has enabled payments, you may be able to pay an invoice through WeProject.

Payments are taken by Stripe. The Business connects its own Stripe account, and your payment is made to that account and settles directly to the Business. Ouitech does not receive, hold or control those funds, and is not a party to your contract with the Business. We are not a payment services provider, and we are not supplying the goods or services being invoiced.

Your card details do not reach us. Card and bank details are entered directly into Stripe’s payment fields. Ouitech does not receive or store your full card number.

What we do record about the payment: your name and email address, the invoice or quotation reference, the amount and currency, the payment method type, the last four digits of a card, the payment status, the date and time, Stripe’s transaction references, and records of failed payments, refunds and disputes.

Stripe’s own role. Stripe processes payment information as an independent controller for its own purposes, including fraud prevention, anti-money-laundering and sanctions checks, and regulatory reporting. It does not act on our instructions for those purposes. Stripe’s handling of your information is governed by Stripe’s own privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy.

Questions and refunds. Contact the Business that issued the invoice. Refunds are issued by it, from its own Stripe account. We cannot reverse a payment.

8. RECORDS OF YOUR APPROVALS

When you accept, approve, confirm or reject a quotation in the Portal, we create a record of that action. This is deliberate: the record is what allows both you and the Business to establish later what was agreed and when.

The record includes your user account, the quotation or project concerned, the action you selected, the date and time, your IP address, and related transaction references.

These records are available to the Business. They are kept for the term of the Business’s subscription and then for six years, because they evidence a commercial transaction. They may be used for dispute resolution, audit, security or legal purposes.

The legal effect of accepting a quotation is a matter between you and the Business, and is governed by the Business’s own terms.

9. WHO YOUR INFORMATION IS SHARED WITH

The Business that invited you, and the people it authorises to manage your project. It can see the information you submit and a record of the actions you take in the Portal, including that you logged in, opened a quotation, responded to it, uploaded a document or completed an approval.

Our service providers, who help us operate the Portal — cloud hosting, infrastructure, databases, authentication, security, email delivery, monitoring, support tooling and backups. They act on our instructions under written contracts and may not use your information for their own purposes.

Stripe, in relation to payments, as an independent controller — see section 7.

Professional advisers, and public authorities where required or permitted by law, and in connection with a corporate transaction. Section 10 of the Ouitech Ltd Privacy Policy has the detail.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising. There is no third-party advertising in WeProject.

11. HOW LONG INFORMATION IS KEPT

Where we hold information on the Business’s behalf, it is kept for the term of the Business’s subscription and then deleted or returned within 30 days of the end of it, unless the Business instructs otherwise or the law requires us to keep it. The Business’s own retention decisions govern its copy of the information.

Approval and acceptance records are kept as described in section 8.

Where we are the controller, our standard periods are in section 13 of the Ouitech Ltd Privacy Policy. In summary: security logs 12 months, system logs 90 days, support correspondence 36 months, payment records 6 years.

After information is deleted from our live systems it may remain in backups until those expire in the ordinary cycle.

No system connected to the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please keep your password and login details confidential. Section 14 of the Ouitech Ltd Privacy Policy has the full description.

If we ever introduce a cookie that is not strictly necessary, we will ask for your consent before setting it and update this notice.

International transfers. Some of our service providers process information outside the United Kingdom. Where that happens we put a lawful transfer mechanism in place. Section 12 of the Ouitech Ltd Privacy Policy explains which mechanisms we use.
